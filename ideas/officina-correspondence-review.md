# Correspondence Trials in Officina

## 15 September 2026

The owner requested at least three sequential notebook runs, an editorial review of each output, prompt improvements when warranted, and an authored comparison example. The study is recorded in [the Officina comparison](../officina/comparison-002-004.md). These are synthetic model-path runs, not observations of an adolescent's engagement.

Iteration 002 stopped after its first returned page. Both continuation responses were exactly 9,000 characters and ended inside a JSON string. The router slices text to `request.max_output_chars`; the continuation budget was 9,000 while the deviser already allowed 20,000. The continuation now allows 20,000 characters. This permits the richer document already requested and costs a larger possible response; it does not relax the parsed format or safety gate. The focused transport test exercises a response longer than the former ceiling.

The first page asked somebody to choose between "metà", "otto tratti", "due impronte" and "un varco", then write a pact and identify a deviation, without explaining those operations. The illustration consumed more space than the drawing area. The returned synthetic page invented material instead of demonstrating that these instructions were understood. The deviser prompt now asks for executable choice wording, concrete actions before methodological explanations, and continuity of names, positions and rules across pages. This adds instruction length but leaves the subject and creative choices open.

Iteration 003 used those changes with the same household and brief. It passed parsing but stopped on an existing text heuristic that rejected slash-separated words copied from the synthetic return. Its first page combined three drawings, two symbols, a legend and a limitation of the code. The revised wording made individual drawing requests clearer but did not distribute the work well. The large decorative map was unused while drawings occupied smaller boxes.

Before 004, the deviser was asked to distribute work across exchanges and make the map itself the working surface. The continuer was asked to distinguish written meanings from reported symbol shapes and to express slash-separated alternatives in prose. This keeps the current check in place, but it remains brittle: a valid quotation can be rejected as unfinished wording. A prompt change cannot guarantee that the heuristic will never reject another valid continuation.

Method sampling varies, so a better result cannot establish which change caused it. Failed notebooks retain their error outputs and gained separate editorial review cells. The budget regression fails with the former limit and passes with the new one. The final focused check passes 69 tests covering workbench operations, handwriting, continuation context, repair, safety, rendered prompts and existing gate callers; ruff also passes. All five new notebooks validate structurally and retain their execution outputs. The authored example's four display messages fit four lines of at most 44 characters; its pages remain unrendered and untested.

Iteration 004 concluded, but its first truth choice had no proposition, its route task was underspecified and its ending depicted a route only claimed in writing. The prompts now ask for complete statements and supported route rules and distinguish written claims from visible work. Iteration 005 then exposed the safety service's independent 10,000-code-point request limit. The shared gate now examines 10,000-character windows with 512-character overlap and takes each category's maximum severity. Every character is covered; failures prevent a seal. The overlap is an engineering choice for local boundary context, not an empirically validated safety guarantee. It adds screening calls and cannot preserve unlimited context across a long document.

Iteration 006 used the same prompts and the corrected gate. It concluded but delivered only two returns, below the requested three, and its final message was prewritten before the last contribution. The existing notebook assertions accept this. The study therefore records technical completion separately from adherence to the brief. The [authored example](../officina/authored-example.md) is an editorial candidate with complete text and hypothetical replies, not another measured run. No production deployment was performed.
