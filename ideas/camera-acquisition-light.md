# Acquisition light

On 9 September 2026 the owner requested that the LED remain on throughout image acquisition and switch off only when framing can change. The former 150 ms acknowledgement pulse ended independently of framebuffer readiness and therefore could not serve that purpose.

The firmware now turns GPIO5 on in startWork before scheduling capture. The worker clears an atomic acquisition flag after esp_camera_fb_get returns the selected framebuffer; the LED loop then switches the light off and starts a minimum 500 ms dark interval before subsequent feedback. Saving, byte verification and upload proceed independently of framing. Two 150 ms flashes still require the matching durable hub receipt. Capture and task-creation failures release the acquisition light and retain the three-flash error path. Off means acquisition has ended, not that persistence succeeded, and power must remain connected during saving and delivery. No debounce, pin assignment, TLS or queue-format change was made.

The isolated camera build succeeded and revision camera-2026-09-09-capture-led was installed with flash hash and fresh authenticated telemetry checks. An explicit diagnostic USB capture on camera 94:A9:90:D0:9D:D0 observed GPIO5 HIGH during capture, then LOW after framebuffer readiness at 543 ms and before receipt. The JPEG was 39,642 bytes, acquisition plus verified storage took 1,248 ms and upload took 6,617 ms. HTTP 201, matching capture/upload identifier and queue zero followed. USB_TEST_OFF was acknowledged at the end. These are software output-register and device-timing observations, not oscilloscope measurements or proof of image sharpness after physical movement. The owner can now test movement after light-off without waiting for the delivery flashes.
