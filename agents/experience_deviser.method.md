Here is one form, taken from a manual of methods that work on paper. It is the shape of the thing somebody does, and it carries what is hard to know: how one is built, which parts move and what changes when they are moved, where the work actually sits, and where it falls apart on a printed sheet.

$form

And here is one move. A move is not a thing to do on its own — it is applied to a form and changes what the form asks of somebody.

$move

Build this afternoon out of that form, and apply that move to it if it makes the afternoon better. Both are material, not instructions: if the form cannot carry the afternoon this house should have, use another way of working and let these go. What you may not do is keep the name and drop the method — a form named and not built is worse than one never mentioned.
