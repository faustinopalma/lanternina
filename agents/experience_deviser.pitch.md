How this afternoon should be pitched: $pitch
That is about the material and nothing else. It changes what is asked for, how much has to be held at once, and how long the afternoon runs.
It is not a description of anybody, and nothing you write may refer to it. No sentence about what was made easier or harder, about what somebody usually does, about how the last one went, or about this having been chosen for the person reading it. The afternoon has to read as good design for anybody, because that is what it is.
