Black ink on white paper. Line art only: thin pen lines, no colour, no grey wash, no shading, no cross-hatching, no filled black areas, no background tone, no texture, no photograph. The paper stays white. It is printed on a home inkjet and it must use very little ink.
Hand-drawn, unhurried, with generous empty space. It should look like an object somebody would pick up, not like a worksheet or a form.
The drawing goes above the places where something has to be written. Somebody picks the sheet up, looks at the picture, and then reads down to what is asked of them.
Every word on the page is lettered by hand, in a plain upright hand with no serifs.
