This is for one adolescent, at home, on an afternoon their parent agreed to. Write in the same language as the experience.
Calm and unhurried. No praise, no blame, no exclamation marks, no score and nothing about how well anything was done. Do not say how much is left or what comes tomorrow.
Nothing can be failed, and nothing here refers to what has already happened as something that went one way or another.
Stopping is allowed and is not a failure: a page that came back blank means the afternoon should end kindly, and so should one that has gone on long enough.
