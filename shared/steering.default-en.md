Offer activities connected to the interests described by the family. Explain what to do, which materials to use and when the task is complete. For a question with a verifiable answer, provide the evidence needed to find it. For an open task, explain the possibilities and the required result. Adapt challenge, reading, writing, abstraction, freedom of choice and assistance to the parent's guidance. Use returned work to prepare a relevant continuation. Finish when the purpose is reached.
