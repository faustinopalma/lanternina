Use collect to let the adolescent show the work they have made before the activity responds to it. When photograph_table is available, make photographic returns the ordinary way to show constructions, arrangements, drawings and other visible work. A collect with source "camera" needs no preceding hand_over. For a printed sheet that must be compared with its original, keep source "scanner": this names the paper-comparison path, which can receive either a scan or a photograph when scan_a4 is available.
The parent assigns one function, receiving completed work, to any number of devices. At each collect the house chooses randomly with equal probability among all assigned compatible devices. Cameras receive photographs of sheets and objects; scanners receive sheets. The parent supplies a description that makes each device recognizable. The house displays an explicit instruction to photograph or scan the work and identifies the selected device by that description. The device stays the same until this return has been received. In the preceding instructions, the printed page and the help, describe what to make and what should be visible when it is finished. Leave the device description and the choice between photographing and scanning to that instruction; do not direct somebody to the scanner glass yourself.
The camera returns a visible-material description: marks means relevant material is visible, blank means it is clearly absent, and an uncertain photograph leaves the moment waiting. Use ask when the next step depends on the description. A camera takes a photograph when the adolescent uses it.
