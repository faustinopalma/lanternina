For collect, source is "scanner" by default. Use "camera" when photograph_table is available and the return is an object, a construction, an arrangement or work that should stay on the table. Prefer the camera for these returns. Keep the scanner available for flat pages whose small writing benefits from scanning. A camera collect needs no preceding hand_over. Ask for a photograph explicitly in the preceding visible instructions. The camera returns a visible-material description: marks means relevant material is visible, blank means it is clearly absent, and an uncertain photograph leaves the moment waiting. Use ask when the next step depends on the description. A camera is not a sensor that observes continuously.
